**Landing Page Wireframe Specification (High Fidelity Blueprint)**

**1. Sign-up Page (Lead Generation):**
*   **Goal:** Maximize email capture/conversion.
*   **Structure:** Hero section with a powerful, benefit-driven headline and subhead. Visual representation of the product/service. Single, prominent CTA button ("Start Free Trial" / "Sign Up"). Minimalist form (Email address required, Name optional). Trust signals (security badges, small testimonial slider) placed below the form.
*   **Flow:** Clear path from awareness to action in under 5 seconds.

**2. Download Page (Asset Acquisition/Magnet):**
*   **Goal:** Exchange valuable content for contact information.
*   **Structure:** Headline matching the downloaded asset's value. Brief description of what the user gains from downloading. Embedded form requiring email (and optionally, Name/Company). Visual mockup of the asset itself (e.g., PDF cover image). Clear CTA ("Download Now").
*   **Flow:** Direct and friction-less path to the download link upon form submission.

**3. Contact Page (Direct Inquiry):**
*   **Goal:** Facilitate direct sales/support inquiry.
*   **Structure:** Clear contact options (Form, Email address, Phone number). Brief explanation of response time. Embedded inquiry form tailored to the user's need (e.g., "Sales Inquiry," "Support Question"). Optional map/office address if physical presence is relevant.
*   **Flow:** Guides the user to the most appropriate method of contact based on their need.
